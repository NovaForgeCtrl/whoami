# About Me

I'm not a trained IT professional.

I'm a career changer currently training to become an IT Specialist for System Integration (Fachinformatiker für Systemintegration).

I don't have a job in IT yet, and I don't have years of professional experience to point to.

What I do have is the willingness to learn and the curiosity to figure things out myself.

A lot of my learning happens outside of formal education.

I build systems, configure servers, experiment with networks, test software, break things, fix them and document what I learn.

My homelab has become one of the most important parts of that process.

It gives me a place where I can experiment without being afraid of breaking something — because breaking something is often how I learn the most.

I'm especially interested in Linux, infrastructure, networking, virtualization, security, automation and system administration.

I'm still figuring out where exactly I want to end up in IT.

For now, I'm focused on building the experience I don't have yet.

> I don't have the experience yet.
> So I'm trying to build it.
